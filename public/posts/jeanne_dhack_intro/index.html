CTF experience

12 August 2026

Since two years ago, I’ve had the opportunity to design challenges for the Jeanne d’Hack CTF, a capture the flag competition that takes place in Rouen. I had already participated in CTF’s, but seeing the inside and discovering how the infrastructure is handled is a completely different experience!

Here are some of the challenges I created:

PWN

  • Magic Maze

Steganography

  • A way to hide data in GIF files
  • MP4 “obfuscation”
